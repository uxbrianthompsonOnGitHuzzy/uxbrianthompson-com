Meeting Center.

Intelligence that knows when to speak, when to disappear, and when to ask.

Challenge

The meeting lifecycle is broken at every stage.

Pre-meeting: advisors manually search for client context across 5+ screens. During meeting: technology interrupts rather than supports the conversation. Post-meeting: 30+ minutes of admin to document what happened. No connection between meeting outcomes and active alerts.

What We Built

AI-orchestrated pre, during, and post-meeting intelligence.

Meeting Center transforms the entire meeting lifecycle. Before: auto-loaded prep with confidence-labeled talking points. During: restrained AI that captures silently. After: structured extraction with one-tap approval and auto-drafted follow-ups.

Pre-Meeting

Time-adaptive prep

24hr = full context. 10 min = top 3 bullets. Walking in = single key insight. Each talking point shows source + confidence.

During Meeting

Restrained AI

UI strips to capture-only. Timer + minimal notes. No sidebar, no suggestions. Voice capture runs silently.

Post-Meeting

Structured extraction

Action items + sentiment + topics. Each field shows confidence. Draft follow-up auto-generated. One-tap approval.

Key Decisions

Design choices that defined the experience.

  • Time-adaptive intelligence: The closer to the meeting, the more focused the prep — from full dossier to single key insight
  • Restrained AI during meetings: The most important design choice is knowing when to disappear — no suggestions, no interruptions
  • Structured extraction over freeform: Post-meeting notes are parsed into relationship, financial goals, and action items — not a wall of text
  • Agentforce-native actions: Every follow-up creates real Salesforce records — cases, life events, tasks, emails — not just notes
