FSC Agentic Wealth Experience

Anywhere Advisor.

Slack-first agentic surface — full action capability from mobile, zero CRM login required.

Challenge

Advisors aren’t at their desk when signals fire.

Wealth advisors spend their days in conference rooms, between client sites, on branch floors, or traveling. When a high-priority alert fires — a large money movement, a portfolio in drift, a client flagged for attrition — the advisor may not see it for hours.

The challenge: bring the intelligence to the advisor’s mobile surface of choice, with full action capability, without requiring a CRM login.

What We Built

Three pillars of Slack-native intelligence.

Run My Day DM

Proactive daily brief

Each morning, the agent proactively DMs the advisor with the three highest-priority signals. Inline actions: Acknowledge, View Client, Dismiss, Schedule Meeting.

Client 360 Alerts

Real-time intelligence

Contextual alerts throughout the day when signals reach threshold. Drill Down opens full Client 360 modal directly in Slack — Net Worth, Cash, Allocation, Actions.

Meeting Center

Channel-based orchestration

Every meeting gets a dedicated channel. Pre: AI context brief. During: voice recording. Post: AI summary, auto-drafted tasks, “Commit to Salesforce” button.

Key Decisions

Design choices that defined the experience.

  • Slack as first-class surface: Not a notification relay — complete workflows execute without returning to CRM
  • Block Kit as design system: Rich Slack components carry the interaction complexity. Entire UI lives in Block Kit JSON.
  • IF/THEN rule builder: Admins configure agent intelligence without code — abstracts prompt engineering behind condition/action patterns
  • Meeting channels as persistent context: Each meeting gets a channel that becomes the source of truth. All participants auto-added.
  • Proactive by design: The agent initiates. Advisors don’t pull information — the system pushes the right signal at the right moment.
