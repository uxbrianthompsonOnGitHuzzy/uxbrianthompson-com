Challenge

AI guidance at enterprise scale.

Erica serves tens of millions of customers with varying financial literacy, risk tolerance, and engagement patterns. The challenge was designing conversational AI patterns that feel personal and helpful without being intrusive or anxiety-inducing — especially around sensitive financial topics like declining balances, missed payments, or investment losses.

Every AI recommendation carries real financial consequences. The system needed to build trust through transparency, offer guidance without creating dependence, and know when to step back.
