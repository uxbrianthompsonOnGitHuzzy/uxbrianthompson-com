Audi

Led, conceptualized, and executed UX and brand design and writing for the FWA-awarded site redesign, myAudi mobile experiences, social experiences, and marketing materials across all channels.

Impact
• 227% increase in car configurations
• 675% increase in myAudi interactions
• 2,763% increase in financing page visits
• Directly trackable sales hit an all-time high
Roles
• Creative Direction: Brian Thompson
• Style Guide: Audi / Brian Thompson
• UX Design: Agency / Brian Thompson
• UX Writing: Brian Thompson
PURPOSE
• Allow people to configure, save and order a hyper-custom Audi
• Recreate experiencing our cars in real life using artistry and technology
• Create an emotional connection with Audi vehicles
• Increase myAudi sign-up, education, and engagement over the owner lifecycle
• Reimagine what an automotive site can, and should be — engineered
• Increase vehicle configurations and trackable sales

TAKEAWAY
• People built their ideal Audi and explored key features with unprecedented realism and scale — and ordered it
• People searched live new and pre-owned inventory to find any car quickly, get specific vehicle questions answered accurately, and locate a dealer to order
• People embraced myAudi to unify and manage everything they want to know about their Audi, from maintenance schedules to utilizing innovations

UX design and writing for desktop and mobile
Component Continuity
Using building blocks from the existing Audi guidelines, I created an array of flexible components to create distinct page designs for individual models, technical specifications, finance options, inventory levels, as well as brand campaigns, and seasonal promotions. 
This kept all the designs super consistent and extremely efficient, even with ongoing content additions, updates, and revisions.
Process Flow
Working from the user persona, we created a task flow to find their ideal Audi vehicle, personalize the features, and begin the purchase process. 
• Created a process flow to assist and guide people
• Hyper-customization feature selection capabilities
• Automatically saved vehicles created
• Locate dealer inventory for the vehicle they built
• Provide a better understanding of purchase options
• Invited people to begin the purchase process
