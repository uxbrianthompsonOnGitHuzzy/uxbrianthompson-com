Run My Day.

Detect, analyze, recommend, enable — decision-ready intelligence in under 5 minutes.

Challenge

Preparation is the hidden productivity killer.

Financial advisors check 5+ systems before their first meeting. Critical client signals are buried across disconnected dashboards. Morning prep takes 45+ minutes of manual aggregation — and retention risks are discovered too late.

What We Built

An AI-native morning workspace.

Run My Day synthesizes overnight signals, upcoming meetings, and client risk indicators into a single prioritized view. Four groups organize every insight by advisor intent, not data source.

Retain

Protect the book

Anomalous transactions, churn risk scores, behavioral signals, market volatility response

Grow

Centers of influence, pattern discovery, uninvested cash opportunities

Service & Comply

Maintain trust

Open cases, compliance flags, regulatory deadlines approaching

Key Decisions

Design choices that defined the experience.

  • Intent-based grouping: Retain, Grow, Service, Comply — organized by what the advisor wants to do, not where data lives
  • Confidence labels on every assessment: High/Medium confidence + supporting signal count builds trust
  • Structured dismiss & snooze: Every dismissal trains the model — the system gets smarter with each decision
  • Opportunity/risk framing: Every insight shows what happens if addressed AND if ignored
  • Human-in-the-loop: Every action requires advisor approval — FINRA/SEC compliant by design
  • Progressive disclosure: Group → Insight type → Client list → Full detail — complexity on demand
