Challenge

Before any code was written, I co-led the cross-functional research and strategy effort that defined the 3-year FSC Wealth vision. Then I made it tangible — alone, over a 4th of July weekend — with a prototype that turned a strategy deck into something people could touch, feel, and believe in.

Key Decisions

What made the incubation land.

  • Prototype over presentation: A tangible experience beats a strategy deck. Building it made people believe it was possible, not just desirable.
  • Customer voice as proof: Advisor quotes and validation data became the narrative that sold the investment internally.
  • Connected ecosystem from day one: The prototype showed six products talking to each other — not six separate pitches, one unified vision.
  • Film as alignment tool: Six script iterations produced a narrative that reinvigorated a stalled project and aligned a distributed organization.
  • One designer, full ownership: Prototype, film script, UX writing, customer-facing narrative — all from one hand, ensuring coherence.
