Challenge

Client context is trapped in silos.

A client’s full picture — relationships, life events, financial goals, risk tolerance, communication history, and sentiment — lives across a dozen disconnected records. Advisors spend the first minutes of every interaction re-assembling context that the system already knows.

Key Decisions

Design choices that defined the experience.

  • Narrative over fields: AI generates a readable summary instead of forcing advisors to scan 40+ data fields
  • Life events as triggers: Detection doesn’t just log — it activates. A detected life event fires alerts, updates plans, and suggests next steps.
  • Contextual alerts embedded: Agentforce banners surface directly on the record page — “$300K by 2028” opportunity, “Fixed In” warning
  • Household-first architecture: The system thinks in households, not individuals — shared goals, combined AUM, cross-referenced relationships
  • RAG-grounded responses: All AI outputs grounded in org-specific knowledge via RAG Studio — no hallucination in financial context
